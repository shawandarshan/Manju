Research

Anti-horn system
Research area

Noise pollution and road traffic

Honking is one of the most avoidable sources of noise on Indian roads, and one of the hardest to switch off. A horn is meant to warn. In dense traffic it has become a habit, repeated at signals, junctions, school gates and hospital entrances until the street never goes quiet.

My research treats this as two problems at once: a noise-pollution problem, the sound traffic adds to where people live, study and recover, and a road-traffic problem, the driving behaviour that produces it. The project, a Secure, Intelligent, Portable No-Honking System with Centralized Management, is my attempt to bring that noise down.

Why it matters

01

Road traffic is the most common source of environmental noise. WHO links long-term exposure to annoyance, disturbed sleep and heart disease. Every 10 dB is ten times the sound energy and is heard as roughly twice as loud, so a horn near 100 dB against a 55 dB residential limit is a gap of more than ten thousand times the energy.
